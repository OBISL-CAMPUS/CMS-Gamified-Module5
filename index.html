Dichotomy: Customer vs Consumer

Pending

Marketers must decouple the purchasing transaction (Customer role) from actual user utility (Consumer role). Route the launch parameters for the educational tablet above into Parent or Child criteria.

The Dual Vectors: Decoupling transactional clients from end-users ensures you target the correct emotional triggers in separate messaging maps.

  • Customer (Buyer): Driven by logical values like payment terms, specifications, and educational boundaries.
  • Consumer (User): Activated by responsive, sensory feedback and colorful interactive elements.
Strategy: Parent acts as financial gatekeeper; child is the vector of real physical demand.

Step 04 / 04

Systemic Balance: Fishbein Model

Pending

Quantify qualitative market sentiment. Tap the adjustment circles on the canvas sliders to modify consumer belief ($b_i$) and importance weight ($e_i$). Optimize overall brand attitude score $A_o \ge 175$ to outperform competitors!

The Fishbein Multi-Attribute Model mathematical equation:

$$A_{o} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} (b_{i} \times e_{i})$$

Where:
$A_{o}$ = Brand/Product Attitude Score
$b_{i}$ = Strength of consumer belief that the brand possesses attribute $i$ (1-10)
$e_{i}$ = Importance evaluation weight of attribute $i$ (1-10)
